Sound Probe

Can two phones talk by sound at dueling distance? One phone sends test tones, the other listens and scores them.

How to test

  1. Open this page on both phones (https). Turn the volume up on the sender.
  2. On the listening phone, pick the distance and grip, then tap Listen.
  3. On the sending phone, tap Send within a few seconds. Leave it sending.
  4. Hold both phones as in a duel (pointed at the ground) until the listener shows results (about 20 s).
  5. Repeat at 1 m, 3 m and 5 m, then tap Copy all results and paste them back.
